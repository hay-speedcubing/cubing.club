Reference

The method, step by step

A quick reference for the layer-by-layer method shown in the video — come back to this anytime you get stuck.

  1. Build the white cross Get the four white edge pieces around the white center, matching each one to its neighboring center color.
  2. Solve the white corners Slot each white corner piece into place, completing the first layer.
  3. Solve the middle layer edges Move the four remaining edge pieces (no yellow) into the middle layer.
  4. Make the yellow cross Orient the top layer edges so the yellow face shows a cross.
  5. Orient the yellow corners Get all four top corners showing yellow on top, regardless of position.
  6. Position the yellow corners Move the corners into their correct spots without worrying about the edges yet.
  7. Position the yellow edges Cycle the last edges into place — and the cube is solved.
Watch out for

Common mistakes

Cross pieces flipped A "cross" that looks right from the top can still be wrong — check that each edge color matches its side center too.
Breaking the first layer It's easy to accidentally twist the bottom layer while solving the middle layer. Slow down and double-check before each move.
Mixing up orient vs. position Orienting (which way a piece faces) and positioning (where it goes) are two separate steps — trying to do both at once causes most beginner confusion.
Memorizing without understanding It's fine to memorize the last few steps at first, but come back later and learn *why* they work — it makes everything after this much easier.
