Our mission

Growing a self-sustaining cubing community in Armenia

Cubing Armenia is becoming a WCA-recognized non-profit — built on free community practice, WCA-sanctioned competitions, and coaching for every skill level.

About the NGO →
  • WCA Junior DelegateMayis Jraghatspanyan represents Armenia within the World Cube Association.
  • Non-profit by designCompetition and NGO activity is not-for-profit — any surplus goes back into the community.
  • All of ArmeniaOrganizing and supporting competitions nationwide.
