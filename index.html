Our mission

Growing a self-sustaining cubing community in Armenia

Cubing Armenia exists to grow a self-sustaining puzzle-solving community — through free community practice, WCA-sanctioned competitions, accessible coaching, and, soon, affordable equipment for every cuber, from their first solve to the podium.

We're currently completing registration as an official Armenian non-profit organization, so that competitions, coaching, and community programs can run transparently and grow beyond one person.

  • WCA Junior DelegateMayis Jraghatspanyan represents Armenia within the World Cube Association.
  • Non-profit by designCompetition and NGO activity is not-for-profit — any surplus goes back into the community.
  • Regional reachActive organizing in Armenia
