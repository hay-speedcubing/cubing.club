About Cubing Armenia

A WCA-recognized non-profit, built to grow speedcubing in Armenia.

Cubing Armenia exists to build a self-sustaining puzzle-solving community — through free community practice, WCA-sanctioned competitions, coaching, and a fair, transparent organization behind it all.

Our mission

More cubers, more competitions, more fun — in Armenia

Our goal is simple: more people solving, more official competitions, more chances to compete close to home — all run fairly, openly, and for the benefit of the community rather than any one person.

We believe speedcubing has real value beyond the puzzle itself — it builds focus, problem-solving, and confidence, especially in young people. That's why our weekly meetups are free, and why we're building this as a non-profit rather than a private club.

The story so far

From one delegate to a national community

Cubing Armenia is led by Mayis Jraghatspanyan, WCA Junior Delegate for Armenia, who has represented the country within the World Cube Association while organizing and supporting competitions nationwide.

What started as informal weekend meetups has grown into a recognizable community — and the next step is making that official: a properly registered non-profit that can run competitions, coaching, and outreach transparently, and outlast any one organizer.
